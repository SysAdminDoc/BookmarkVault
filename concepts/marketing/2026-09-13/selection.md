# Brand selection

## Decision

Keep the existing purple and pink bookmark identity, then rebuild it as a clean vector master. It already fits the product, reads immediately as a saved-link tool, and carries the same accent colors as the interface.

## Improvements made

- Replaced the inline interface glyph with a reusable SVG logo.
- Balanced the bookmark stroke and the rounded-square silhouette for small sizes.
- Exported transparent 512 px and 64 px PNG assets from the same master.
- Tested the mark at favicon size and against the app's dark background.

## Quality review

The selected mark stays recognizable at 16 px, has clean transparent edges, and doesn't rely on fine detail. A new visual direction wasn't needed. The stronger move was to refine the established identity and use it consistently across the product, repository, and release artwork.

The README hero uses real product captures. The desktop view is cropped so its small interface version badge does not appear in the hero artwork.
